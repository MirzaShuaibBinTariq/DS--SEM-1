Error-Detecting Codes

Error-Detecting Codes are used in computer systems to help detect errors that may occur when data is stored or transmitted.

Course Note:

Why Error Detection is Needed

When data is transferred or stored, an error may sometimes occur. Error-detecting techniques are used to identify whether the received or stored data has been changed or corrupted.
